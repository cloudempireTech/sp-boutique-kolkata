About the boutique

A place to find your own way to dress up.

S P Boutique is a fashion boutique in Ram Bagan, Kolkata, associated with sarees, blouses, lehengas, gowns and custom clothing.

Our point of view

Start with what feels like you.

Some days call for a saree you can return to again and again. Others ask for a lehenga, a blouse that brings a look together, or a gown for a special event. The right piece begins with your occasion and your own sense of style.
